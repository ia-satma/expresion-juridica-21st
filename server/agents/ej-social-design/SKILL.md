# EJ social art direction

This reviewed adaptation uses design principles from Anthropic's frontend-design and canvas-design skills, Impeccable's typeset/layout/colorize/polish modules, and the inspection procedure from Anthropic's webapp-testing. Impeccable is pinned to 9fadc41729c38fd47d7ae622b501558653f9d2e9; Anthropic sources are pinned to ca45fc496a3436251f191354091d557da31c15b5. Apply the adapted instructions below, not conflicting host commands in archived originals. The application's EJ brand, requested format, exact piece count, structured response schema, facts and user choices govern the result. Use the configured Sol model for art direction and the configured image model, default GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst, for visual assets. Keep native lettering, typography and the original logo separate from generated bitmaps.

## Start from the message

Identify the subject, audience, intended network and one useful takeaway. Let the actual material determine the visual treatment: a real process can use steps, an attributed quotation can use a quote composition, a human activity can use documentary photography. Do not decorate every topic with the same layout. Never invent statistics, quotations, legal authorities, results or claims to make a design look interesting.

Choose a coherent art direction before returning the structured design: an approved palette, a type hierarchy, a focal point, photographic emphasis, spacing and a role for each piece. Compare composition alternatives against the actual amount of content and choose the one that communicates best. Return final decisions in the supplied schema; do not output a manifesto, planning transcript or a new output format.

## Compose inside the existing EJ system

Use the original supplied logo, Libre Caslon Text Italic headings and Bahnschrift Light body. Choose among approved navy/gold, white editorial, ivory/navy and graphite/gold families. A fixed user palette wins; mixed palettes must feel like one campaign. Gradients are allowed: tonal uses mapped EJ linear tones, glow uses a restrained mapped radial treatment, solid is flat and auto lets the compositor choose. Let the background support hierarchy or photographic atmosphere. Use only these supported brand treatments, without unrelated colors, replacement fonts or redrawn logos.

Give each piece one dominant visual idea, carried by a photograph, statement or genuine sequence. Establish unmistakable title/body/metadata roles through scale, placement and space. Keep related elements close and distinct groups separated. A longer title needs a better arrangement and more room, not a wall of small text. Preserve consistent optical margins, readable contrast and the gold thread. Use the plain or curved panel control to organize the composition when it reflects the brand references.

Design for graphic body text around 40 px on the 1080 px logical canvas, with 36 px as the minimum. Do not rely on tiny text to fit a narrow column. Prefer a concise headline, a short body or one/two useful bullets, rather than combining all of them. Move explanations, complete URLs and detailed citations to network copy and source metadata. A short source name may be printed when needed. Preserve the full meaning, qualifications and source traceability in the copy. Do not repeat the headline as body copy or add a redundant label. Numbering communicates actual steps or carousel position; a standalone post remains complete without a decorative ordinal.

For a carousel, establish a strong cover, connected middle and useful closing. Preserve the requested count. Vary composition when the message benefits while keeping type roles, logo treatment and the gold thread coherent. Use imagePosition left/right/top/background for a deliberate reading path; imageShare dominant lets the image lead, balanced gives image and text comparable weight. Avoid making every piece the same pair of columns. Choose left alignment for paragraphs; center can serve a sparse display statement. The supported design controls are the rendering vocabulary; do not promise unsupported arbitrary graphics or change the brand to create variation.

## Respect the destination

Use the supplied dimensions and network guidance. A horizontal graphic needs substantially less text; a thumbnail needs a clear focal point, not a reduced document. Portrait graphics still need to read on a phone. Keep story/TikTok essentials inside runtime safe margins. A PNG is not a video, Reel or automatic social-media publication.

Use documentary-style photographs of contemporary Mexico unless the material names a different location. Relevant people may appear with realistic proportions and credible activity. Generated images remain illustrative, with the runtime's visible IA caption. Keep lettering, logos and editable text out of generated photographs. Give human subjects and the central photographic action room; do not cover faces with body text.

## Review and refine

Before returning, review hierarchy, concise wording, breathing room, mobile reading, photo placement and the campaign's rhythm. Refine composition rather than adding decoration. Preserve facts and the selected brand. Keep body contrast at least 4.5:1 and large display text at least 3:1; the compositor validates contrast and space with actual brand fonts. Gradient text regions need reliable contrast across the entire area, not only at one end of a gradient.

When supplied with measured overflow, collision or fit diagnostics, repair the named problem through the supported layout/design controls and concise graphic wording. Keep exact piece count and preserve substantive meaning in captions. Never return the same crowded arrangement with smaller and smaller type. When supplied with actual rendered artwork, inspect that artwork before claiming the composition was checked. Without an image, make design decisions but do not claim to have inspected a screenshot or run a browser. The runtime must check rendered bounds, text intersections, font readiness and HTML/PNG agreement; a prompt cannot replace those checks.

When redesigning, inspect the supplied rendered reference images together with their structured content. Improve the layout, palette, spacing and headline scale through the supported controls. Preserve all existing words, photographs and captions. Explain the concrete visual improvements briefly in the user's language. The runtime creates a new alternative and retains the original.

The output remains editable HTML text and graphic elements with matching PNG exports.
